The Risk is Real

GDPR Is Actively Fining Small Businesses

The EU is no longer only targeting large corporations. Data Protection Authorities across Europe are increasingly issuing fines to small and medium businesses — most of whom didn't even know they were non-compliant.

⚠️

One GDPR fine can cost more than years of compliance. The average fine for a small business starts at €5,000 and can reach €20 million for serious violations.

🍪

Missing Cookie Consent

Non-compliant or absent cookie banners are the #1 GDPR violation across Europe.

Fines from €5,000+
📄

Invalid Privacy Policy

A generic copy-paste policy isn't enough — it must reflect your actual data practices.

Frequently fined violation
📧

Unlawful Email Marketing

Sending newsletters without verifiable consent records is a top DPA complaint across the EU.

Fines from €2,000+
🔐

No Data Processing Agreement

Using Google Analytics, Mailchimp, or Stripe without a DPA in place is a mandatory violation.

Required by GDPR Art. 28

Does my small business really need GDPR compliance?+
Yes. GDPR applies to every business that processes personal data of EU residents, regardless of company size. This includes your website contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, analytics tracking, and any customer data you store.
How long does it take to become GDPR compliant?+
With SustainerTech, we complete the full compliance process in 5–7 business days. The free audit alone is completed within 24 hours of your request.
What is included in the GDPR Certificate?+
The certificate confirms that your website and data practices have been audited and brought into compliance with GDPR. It includes a compliance checklist, audit date, and our company details. It can be shared with clients, partners, or regulators.
Do you offer payment plans?+
Yes. For packages €799 and above we offer flexible payment options — typically 50% upfront and the remainder upon delivery. Contact us to discuss what works best for you.
What happens if GDPR laws change after my compliance?+
With Full Guard Plus and Complete Business packages, we monitor GDPR updates for 1 year and update your policies accordingly at no extra cost. For other packages, we offer updates at a reduced rate.
